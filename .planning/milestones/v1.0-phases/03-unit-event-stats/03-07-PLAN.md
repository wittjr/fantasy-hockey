## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): User can judge each last-game unit (EV, PP, SH; forwards and defense) by its shots, goals and shot attempts for and against, with counts that reconcile with the official boxscore. (Not in user-story form; quoted verbatim as in Phases 1 and 2.)

<decisions_made_in_planning>
- Constants start at MAX_CONCURRENT_GAMES = 3 and GAME_START_GAP_MS = 500 (research: about 4 api-web requests per second; measured safe at 3.5/s). Plan 03-09 measures and tunes them (its decisions carry the tuning order and the stop-and-report fallback).
- Split at the user's 60k-token per-plan budget (2026-10-13): this plan was 03-06 and held three tasks at 65k. Its Task 3 (the cold Last 20 measurement that tunes the pacing constants) moved, unchanged in content, to plan 03-09 with the in-app cold and warm Last 20 check, so the DATA-01 live evidence sits in one plan. This plan keeps Tasks 1 and 2 with every rule and test. Estimate: about 20k fixed per plan plus about 17k for Task 1 and 16k for Task 2.
- A thrown network error (connect timeout) is not retried. DATA-01 asks for a 429 retry; a failed game is already left out and named (D-04) and a reload retries it. This is the minimum-code choice among the research suggestions (CONTEXT: retry/backoff details are Claude's discretion).
- Game age comes from the schedule's `startTimeUTC`, not `gameDate` (research "TTL tier"; CONTEXT discretion item).
- Play-by-play is about 140 KB, under Next's 2 MB per-entry Data Cache limit (research), so the raw response is cached; no trimmed copy is needed (CONTEXT discretion item).
</decisions_made_in_planning>
