## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): User can open any player from the research page and see who that player has been on ice with, game by game and by situation, so a change in linemates is obvious. (Not in user-story form; quoted verbatim, see 02-01.)

<objective>
Add the panel controls. The header shows the player's deployment numbers (D-05). EV/PP/SH tabs switch situation without losing the scroll position (D-10). A legend and an amber missing-chart footnote explain the strip (D-24, SC5), and every empty case gets a clear message instead of an empty table.

Purpose: with the tracer in place a user can see a strip, but cannot switch situation from the page, read his TOI/PP trend next to the linemate change, or tell why a strip is empty. This plan closes those gaps for PLYR-02 (situations) and PLYR-03 (denominator stated).
Output: `emptyReason`, `noChartDates` and `SITUATION_WORDS` in the view-model, and the header stats, tabs, intro, legend, footnote and empty states in `components/PlayerLinemates.tsx`, with tests.
</objective>
