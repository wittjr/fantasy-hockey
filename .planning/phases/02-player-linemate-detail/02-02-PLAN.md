## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): User can open any player from the research page and see who that player has been on ice with, game by game and by situation, so a change in linemates is obvious. (Not in user-story form; quoted verbatim, see 02-01.)

<objective>
Give the heat strip its popover (D-07), using one popover component for both strips, and pin the panel's markup with fixture tests. Click, hover or keyboard focus on a square then shows minutes together, the share of the player's situation time and the player's situation time (SC2 "minutes shown on click", PLYR-03).

Purpose: 02-01's tracer renders the panel and puts the facts in each square's aria-label. This plan adds the visible popover, extracts the Per game strip's popover into `StripPopover` so the two strips cannot drift (UI-SPEC "Heat-strip cells and popover"), and locks the panel's states, groups, notice and close link in tests that later plans extend. It was split out of 02-01 to keep that plan under 15 files.

</objective>
