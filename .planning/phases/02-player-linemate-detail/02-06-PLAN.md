## Flagged Assumptions

- Edge probe PLYR-04 (`unclassified` → left unresolved, surfaced here): the tag rules are the RESEARCH Pattern 3 decision table on rounded pooled shares. Real-data firing rates (about a third of EV rows tagged at ±15) are in RESEARCH Q3c. Per D-27, ±15 is kept as one constant to retune in UAT.
- RESEARCH A4: Out means the linemate dressed in none of the recent-part team games. A linemate who dressed but has no counted recent game (for example, no chart) gets no tag.
- RESEARCH A5 / Open Question 2: two tags-unavailable reasons beyond the UI-SPEC copy, `no-earlier-data` (research's copy) and `no-recent-data` (planner copy), cover windows where the comparison has no counted data on one side. That is common this season, when about 38% of games had no shift chart.
- Open Question 3 (CONTEXT-adopted): a linemate returning from injury with real recent time counts as New, the same as a newcomer.
- D-26 (Open Question 5 resolved by the user): tagged rows bypass the cap; "Showing n of total" counts them in n.
