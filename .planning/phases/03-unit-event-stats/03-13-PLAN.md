## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): User can judge each last-game unit (EV, PP, SH; forwards and defense) by its shots, goals and shot attempts for and against, with counts that reconcile with the official boxscore.

## Gaps being closed

**G-03-7 (cosmetic, UAT test 7):** the dash between names sits right in front of the next name, with a wide gap before it. **Root cause confirmed** (the UAT diagnosis left it open): `PlayerLink` renders a `PendingLink`, which is `inline-flex gap-1.5` with an always-rendered 12px spinner (`size-3`, opacity 0) after the name. Every linked name therefore carries about 18px of invisible trailing space before the separator text. Phase 2 accepted this slot (02-UI-SPEC E5 overflow row: "spinner slots add about 18px per name"). The planner rebuilt the markup in WebKit and measured 20.9px before the dash and 3.7px after it. The UAT's suggested fix alone (mx-1 on the separator, no literal spaces) would leave the 18px slot in place, so this plan also stops the spinner from taking inline space in unit tables.

**G-03-3 (cosmetic, UAT test 3):** the +/- and SF% numbers touch the next divider, and the gap after each divider is too large. **Measured cause:** stat cells are `pl-3` only, right-aligned, at fixed `w-12`/`w-14` widths. The planner measured in WebKit that cells keep those widths with border-box sizing, so the visible gap after a divider is width minus right padding minus text width. Left padding has no visible effect. The last number of a group is flush with the next divider because right padding is 0. Reducing left padding, as the UAT suggests, therefore changes nothing on its own. What fixes both symptoms is a uniform right padding on every stat cell. It moves every number 12px left: 12px of space before each divider, about 12px less after it, and unchanged spacing between numbers inside a group (measured: after-divider gap for "11" fell from 35px to 23px, and +/- moved from 0px to 12px before the divider).

**Decision references:** D-09 (grouped Goals/Shots/Corsi columns with light dividers) and D-10 (neutral numbers) are kept. Column order, widths, dividers and colors do not change.
