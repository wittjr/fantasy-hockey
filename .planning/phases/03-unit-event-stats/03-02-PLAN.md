## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): User can judge each last-game unit (EV, PP, SH; forwards and defense) by its shots, goals and shot attempts for and against, with counts that reconcile with the official boxscore. (Not in user-story form; quoted verbatim as in Phases 1 and 2.)

<objective>
Give every game's event data two honest reliability signals, computed where the events are attributed: the share of events whose shift-derived on-ice counts agree with play-by-play `situationCode` (D-16, D-17), and a corrupted-shift-chart flag (D-23). The page (plan 03-04) shows counts with an amber warning when either fires; nothing is hidden.

Purpose: DATA-02/DATA-03 reliability. A per-game match threshold catches a missing period or team, and the D-23 flag catches charts like 2025020565 that a match ratio cannot (research Pitfall 5).
Output: new exports and two new `GameEvents` fields in `lib/research/events.ts`, with fixture tests.
</objective>
