## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): User can judge each last-game unit (EV, PP, SH; forwards and defense) by its shots, goals and shot attempts for and against, with counts that reconcile with the official boxscore.

## Gap being closed

**G-03-1 (major, UAT test 1).** The user compared MTL's Oct 13 game (2026020092) with other line-stat sites: line 1 (Suzuki, Caufield, Slafkovský) EV CF is 10 here and 11 elsewhere; line 3 (Kreider, Anderson, Newhook) EV CA is 5 here and 6 elsewhere. Diagnosed root cause: the carried-forward exclusion in 03-CONTEXT.md (line 21, repeated in D-14 and D-21) drops every blocked shot whose `details.reason` is `teammate-blocked` from all counts. Line 1 was on ice 5v5 for MTL's teammate-blocked attempt at P1 03:06, line 3 for BUF's at P2 19:34. The planner re-ran this on live data before writing the plan: current code gives L1 CF 10 and L3 CA 5, and the game has three teammate-blocked plays (P1 03:06 MTL 1551, P1 13:25 MTL 1451, P2 19:34 BUF 1551).

**User decision (2026-10-14, recorded in 03-UAT.md):** count teammate-blocked attempts in CF/CA: CF for the shooter's team (`eventOwnerTeamId`), CA for the opponent, never SF or GF. This supersedes the teammate-blocked part of the D-14/D-21 exclusions. Shootout and penalty-shot exclusions, the pulled-goalie routing to Other (D-21, D-22) and the SF reconciliation are unchanged.

**Evidence the direction is right.** Across all 8 committed fixtures, all 26 teammate-blocked plays have `eventOwnerTeamId` equal to the shooting player's team, so mapping them like any other block credits the shooter's team. Blocked shots never reach SF (only goal and shot-on-goal do), so the boxscore SOG reconciliation cannot move.
