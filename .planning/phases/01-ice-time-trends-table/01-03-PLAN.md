## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): User can scan a team's ice-time section as a trends table and immediately see which players are gaining or losing TOI and power-play time.

<objective>
Add the "who is moving" signal: a recent-vs-window delta for TOI and for PP share on every row, a muted whole-window slope arrow after each delta, green/red colour and a row tint for big movers (±2:00 TOI, ±15 PP-share points), and a "DNP last 2" badge, with the minimum-games and 1-game-window rules that keep noise from looking like a role change.

Purpose: this is the core value of the milestone — spotting players whose deployment just jumped or dropped (TRND-03), with TRND-04's small-sample gates.
Output: delta/mover logic in `lib/research/toi-trends.ts` (+ tests), signed formatters in `lib/research/format.ts`, new columns and states in `components/ToiTrends.tsx` (+ markup tests).
</objective>
