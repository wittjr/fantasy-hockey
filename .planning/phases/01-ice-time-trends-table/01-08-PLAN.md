What the cause turned out to be. In the user's Safari the committed hover/focus popover works: `(hover: hover)` is true, every square carries the markup, the CSS rule is loaded, and the popover appears when the pointer rests on a square. The failure was discoverability and target size. The 12x16 squares, with 1px gaps and 4px corners, read as one line; nothing marked them as interactive; and the user was not landing on or recognising them. Touch screens have no hover, so tapping needs a click path of its own. As a secondary defect, an open popover covers the squares in the next ~4 rows of its column (debug session, Evidence 01:55).

<objective>
Let a click or tap open a Per game square's exact values, keep hover and keyboard focus as extras, stop an open popover from blocking the squares below it, and prove all of it in WebKit (desktop and emulated touch) on the real page.

Purpose: TRND-02 (exact per-game TOI/PP/SH reachable) needs a way in that does not rely on finding a tiny square by hover. That means an explicit click or tap, which is the only way in on touch screens.
Output: new `components/ToiStripCell.tsx` (minimal client wrapper), `components/ToiGameStrip.tsx` wiring and popover classes, markup tests, and a re-runnable `webkit-strip-check.cjs` in the phase directory.
</objective>

## Flagged Assumptions

- **Mechanism.** A per-square client wrapper (`ToiStripCell`) was chosen over native details/summary, the Popover API, and relying on a tap focusing the square (reasons in Task 1). This reverses the UI-SPEC's "CSS-only, no client component" rule for the strip, under the touch backstop that the UI-SPEC and RESEARCH already allowed. Plan 01-09 records it in the UI-SPEC.
- **No Escape key.** It is not part of the gap, so it is deliberately omitted. Keyboard users get the popover from focus and dismiss it by moving focus. A click-opened popover closes on a second click, an outside click or tap, or blur.
- **`role="img"` kept on a clickable square.** The aria-label already carries every value and the popover is an aria-hidden visual duplicate, so opening it gives assistive tech nothing new. `aria-expanded` is not added: role img does not support it (`jsx-a11y/role-supports-aria-props`).
- **Two popovers at once.** If one popover is click-opened and the mouse hovers another square, both show. This is accepted: the click-opened one closes on the next click anywhere.
- **WebKit check (optional per coordinator, included).** It is the only evidence for UAT test 3 until a phone is available. It runs against an auth-free scratch copy bound to 127.0.0.1, uses the `playwright-core` already in the npx cache, and installs nothing. It takes a few minutes (copy + first compile), which is longer than the usual 60-second verify budget; that is accepted for a once-per-plan end-to-end check. It depends on live NHL data for MTL; D12 skips when the window has no OT/SO game.
- **Superseded framing.** The debug session's "Safari ignores hover in an inactive window" explanation is superseded by the user's own Safari check (hover works; the problem was finding and recognising the squares). This plan therefore has no Safari window-activation note.
