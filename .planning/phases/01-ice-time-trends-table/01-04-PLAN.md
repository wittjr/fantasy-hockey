## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): User can scan a team's ice-time section as a trends table and immediately see which players are gaining or losing TOI and power-play time.

<objective>
Make the trends table sortable by Avg TOI, TOI Δ, PP share and PP share Δ through clickable headers, with the sort stored in the URL (`?sort=&dir=`), validated against an allowlist, preserved across reload and team/window switches, and always keeping forwards above defense.

Purpose: sorting by TOI Δ or PP share Δ puts the biggest risers (or fallers) at the top of each group — the fastest way to find pickup/drop candidates (TRND-05).
Output: sort logic in `lib/research/toi-trends.ts` (+ tests), sort headers in `components/ToiTrends.tsx` (+ markup tests), search-param wiring in `app/research/page.tsx`.
</objective>
